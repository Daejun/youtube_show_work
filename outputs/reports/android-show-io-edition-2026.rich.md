## Executive summary

The Android Show: I/O Edition 2026 is a 41-minute Google livestream previewing Android updates ahead of Google I/O 2026. The keynote organizes the news around three focus areas: turning Android from an operating system into an intelligence system, elevating the core Android experience, and Android updates beyond the phone.

The main announcements are Gemini Intelligence, a new generative layer that brings proactive task automation, multimodal assistance, intelligent autofill, the Rambler keyboard feature, and generative UI to Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel later this summer; Android 17 core upgrades including a Meta-built Instagram experience, refreshed emoji, the Pause Point digital wellbeing tool, Quick Share with AirDrop compatibility, and a wireless iOS-to-Android transfer; the next generation of Android Auto with a redesigned UI, premium in-car video and audio, the biggest Google Maps update in over a decade, and Google Built-in expansion to over 100 car models from 16 brands; and the reveal of Google Book, a new laptop category designed for Gemini Intelligence from the ground up, with partners HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASA, and ASUS.

## Topics covered

### Opening and intro from Sar (00:00–04:21)

The opening establishes the broadcast as the Android Show, IO Edition and frames Android as a vital part of Google's mission in what Sar calls the agentic Gemini era.

- Sar thanks the Android community and credits millions of developers and billions of users for the platform.
- Sar frames Android as powering the devices people turn to most for information and answers.
- Sar hands off to Samir Samar, introduced as the president of the Android ecosystem.

### Samir's keynote and three focus areas (04:22–06:55)

Samir opens with momentum stats on Gemini, Google Messages, and Circle to Search, then lays out three focus areas guiding the rest of the show.

- There are now 2.5 billion RCS messages sent every single day.
- Messages across Android and iOS are getting industry-leading end-to-end encryption rolling out.
- The number of group chats across platforms has increased 116%.
- Circle to Search is used billions of times every month for outfit finding, content translation, and travel discovery.
- The three focus areas are: transforming Android from an operating system into an intelligence system; elevating the core Android experience; and Android updates beyond the phone.
- Samir hints at a brand new category coming later in the show.

### Gemini Intelligence introduction (06:55–11:46)

Mindy introduces Gemini Intelligence and walks through proactive task automation and multimodal capabilities, with the rollout starting on the most advanced Android devices.

- Gemini Intelligence brings the best of Gemini to the most advanced Android devices and is exclusive to Android.
- The rollout starts on Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel later this summer and spans phone, watch, car, and glasses.
- Material Expressive is now infused with Gemini Intelligence and animates with purpose to signal transitions from listening to thinking to creating.
- Capabilities have been fine-tuned on Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 devices for the past few months with hand-selected food and ride share apps.
- Mindy demonstrates multimodal capability by snapping a brochure for a coffee tasting tour in Costa Rica and asking Gemini to find a similar tour on Expedia for a group of six.
- Progress on Gemini tasks is visible live from notifications, and tasks stop as soon as they are done.
- Gemini and Chrome bring Chrome desktop automation to Android for tasks like finding a parking spot, updating shipping details, or finding an in-stock item.

### Smart autofill and Rambler keyboard (11:48–14:42)

Deer demonstrates an intelligent autofill that draws on personal context already stored on the phone, then introduces a Gboard feature called Rambler that turns natural speech into structured text.

- Intelligent autofill uses Gemini personal intelligence to fill text boxes on Android apps and in Chrome.
- Deer demonstrates by autofilling passport information from a picture stored in Photos when booking a flight on a new airline.
- Autofill works across Wallet, Gmail, Photos, and more in the Google ecosystem.
- Rambler is a new Gemini Intelligence feature on Gboard that lets users speak naturally and then organizes the text and supports a revise pass for additions like emoji.
- Rambler supports code-switching between multiple languages in a single conversation through Gemini's multilingual model.

### Generative UI and Create my widget (14:42–17:24)

Richie introduces generative UI on Android, starting with widgets users can describe in natural language.

- Create my widget lets users describe a widget in natural language and Gemini Intelligence generates it.
- Richie demonstrates with a widget that suggests three meal prep recipes every week that are high in protein.
- Widgets work across phone and watch — a custom weather widget displays only wind speed and rain.
- More Gemini Intelligence features will roll out later this year.

### Android 17 core experience upgrades (17:29–26:25)

Gabby walks through Android 17 upgrades that improve self-expression, balance, and choice, anchored by a Meta partnership for Instagram, an emoji refresh, the Pause Point digital wellbeing tool, and a smoother iOS-to-Android transfer.

#### Instagram partnership and creator tools

- Google teamed up with Meta to bring the best of Instagram to the most advanced Android devices.
- Capture features include ultra HDR capture and playback, built-in stabilization, and night mode for dark concerts.
- Side-by-side tests confirm video captured and uploaded from Android flagship devices now score the same or better than the leading competitor.
- Instagram is now fully optimized for Android tablets.
- Screen reactions let users drop reactions in front of trending clips, comment sections, or shopping hauls.
- Smart Enhance reveals breathtaking detail and clarity in old photos and videos with a single tap, exclusive to Instagram's edits app on Android.
- Sound separation recognizes and separates audio layers like wind, noise, and music to boost or strip them.
- A "where's this" Android feature is coming to Pixel this summer.

#### Emoji refresh

- Nearly 4,000 emoji have been refined to be richer and more delightful.
- The refreshed emoji roll out later this year on Android devices starting with Pixel across apps like Gboard, YouTube and Gmail.

#### Digital wellbeing — Pause Point

- Pause Point gives a 10-second pause when opening an app marked as distracting.
- During the pause, users can swipe through photos that remind them of what they'd rather be doing.
- Pause Point can suggest alternative apps like an exercise tracker or audiobooks to catch boredom triggers.
- Users can have their phone require a restart before turning off Pause Point for stronger commitment.

#### Sharing and switching

- Quick Share is compatible with AirDrop for Android phones that have the supporting hardware.
- WhatsApp will be able to share instantly using Quick Share.
- The iOS to Android transfer process has been overhauled to wirelessly move passwords, photos, favorite apps, contacts, and even the home screen layout.
- The new transfer launches first on Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel devices later this year.

### Android Auto next generation and Google Built-in (26:35–33:30)

Skimi previews the 2026 Android Auto refresh and the expansion of Google Built-in, with a new adaptive UI, premium entertainment, and Gemini at the center of the in-car experience.

#### Adaptive UI and immersive navigation

- The new Android Auto experience adapts to screens of different shapes and sizes including BMW, Mini, and Lucid vehicles.
- Dashboard widgets give glanceable info while navigating, including shortcuts to favorite contacts, a one-tap garage door opener, and a weather overview.
- Immersive navigation is the biggest update to Google Maps in over a decade, with a vivid 3D view of buildings, overpasses, and terrain.
- Maps highlights critical road details like lanes and stop signs for tricky turns or merges.

#### Premium entertainment

- Android Auto supports video playback in supported cars later this year, including YouTube in 60 frames per second HD video.
- Videos seamlessly transition to audio when it is time to drive.
- Android Auto supports Dolby Atmos in supported apps and cars.
- Many media apps including YouTube Music and Spotify are getting visual upgrades.

#### Gemini in the car

- Gemini is now available in more than 250 million cars that support Android Auto.
- Gemini Intelligence is coming to Android Auto for phones that have it.
- Magic Q can read email and calendar context to send a friend the trail head address with one tap.
- Door Dash is shown as an example of ordering with Gemini in Android Auto.

#### Google Built-in

- Google Built-in is available in over 100 car models from 16 brands.
- Google Built-in apps include Spotify, HBO Max, and Zoom is coming soon.
- Gemini in Google Built-in cars can answer questions tailored to the actual car, such as what a dashboard symbol means or whether a 65-in TV box will fit in the trunk.
- Live lane guidance uses the car's front-facing camera to show the exact lane and guide the next move.

### Google Book reveal (33:34–39:53)

Alex reveals the closing surprise — Google Book — and demonstrates Gemini-driven cursor interactions, generative widgets, cross-device app access, and partner availability.

- Over 15 years ago, Google reinvented laptops for the cloud-first world with the Chromebook.
- Google Books combine the best of Android — a modern OS with powerful apps on Google Play — and the best of Chrome OS, including the world's most popular browser and the biggest library of extensions.
- Google Books are the first laptops designed for Gemini Intelligence from the ground up.
- Magic Pointer is a new cursor that activates with a wiggle and surfaces contextual suggestions like setting up time with friends, drafting a reply, or finding meet-up spots.
- Magic Pointer is multimodal — Alex selects a photo of a nursery and a wallpaper image and a crib image, and Gemini visualizes them together.
- Create my widget is also coming to Google Books, demonstrated with a family reunion planner in Berlin that shows flights, hotel information, restaurant reservations, and a countdown.
- Google Books let users access phone apps directly on the laptop without picking up the phone, demonstrated with a Duolingo lesson.
- Quick Access lets the Google Books file browser show files on a connected phone for moments like attaching a chat-app picture to an email.
- Partners launching Google Book include HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASA, and ASUS.
- Every Google Book will be built with premium craftsmanship and identified by a unique mark called the Global.
- Google Books will be available later this year.

### Closing (39:53–41:06)

Samir closes by tying the updates back to the three focus areas and pointing to the developers cut and Google IO next week.

- These updates are coming to devices with Android 17 this summer, starting with Samsung and Google Pixel.
- A developers cut of the Android show follows the main show.
- Samir invites viewers to join Google for more Android news next week at Google IO.
