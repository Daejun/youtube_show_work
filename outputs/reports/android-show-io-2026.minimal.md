# Watch The Android Show | I/O Edition 2026

## Overview

The Android Show: I/O Edition 2026 is a 41-minute Google livestream previewing Android updates ahead of Google I/O 2026. It covers Gemini Intelligence on Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel, Android 17 core experience upgrades, the next generation of Android Auto, and the reveal of a new laptop category called Google Book.

## Topics covered

- Gemini Intelligence brings the best of Gemini to the most advanced Android devices and rolls out across the ecosystem starting with Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel later this summer, spanning phone, watch, car, and glasses.
- Material Expressive is now infused with Gemini Intelligence and animates with purpose to signal transitions from listening to thinking to creating.
- Gemini Intelligence is being fine-tuned on Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 devices with hand-selected food and ride share apps.
- A multimodal demo shows snapping a brochure for a coffee tasting tour in Costa Rica and asking Gemini to find a similar tour on Expedia for a group of six, with progress visible from notifications.
- Gemini and Chrome bring Chrome desktop automation to Android for tasks like finding a parking spot, updating shipping details, or finding an in-stock item.
- Intelligent autofill uses Gemini personal intelligence to fill text boxes in Android apps and Chrome, demonstrated by pulling passport information from a photo in Photos, and works with Wallet, Gmail, Photos, and more.
- Rambler is a new Gboard feature that lets users speak naturally and have the text organized, and supports code-switching between multiple languages in a single conversation.
- Generative UI in Gemini Intelligence introduces Create my widget, where users describe a widget in natural language, with demos of a high-protein recipe widget and a wind-and-rain weather widget that works across phone and watch.
- Android 17 elevates core experiences across expression, balance, and connection.
- Google teamed up with Meta to bring the best of Instagram to the most advanced Android devices, with ultra HDR capture and playback, built-in stabilization, night mode, and screen reactions.
- Side-by-side tests show video captured and uploaded from Android flagship devices scores the same or better than the leading competitor, and Instagram is now fully optimized for Android tablets.
- Smart Enhance is an Android-exclusive feature in Instagram's edits app that improves the quality of old photos and videos with a single tap.
- Sound separation recognizes and separates audio layers like wind, noise, and music so users can boost or strip them.
- Nearly 4,000 emoji have been refined, rolling out later this year on Android devices starting with Pixel across apps like Gboard, YouTube and Gmail.
- Pause Point gives a 10-second pause when opening an app marked as distracting, with optional photo swipes and alternative app suggestions, and an option to require a phone restart before turning it off.
- Quick Share is now compatible with AirDrop for Android phones with supporting hardware, and WhatsApp will be able to share instantly using Quick Share.
- The iOS to Android transfer process has been overhauled to wirelessly move passwords, photos, favorite apps, contacts, and even the home screen layout, launching first on Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel later this year.
- The next generation of Android Auto launches in 2026 with a new experience that adapts to screens in BMW, Mini, and Lucid, dashboard widgets for contacts, garage door opener, and weather, and immersive navigation in Google Maps with a 3D view of buildings, overpasses, and terrain.
- Android Auto supports premium entertainment with video playback in supported cars later this year, including YouTube in 60 frames per second HD, with seamless transition to audio when driving, plus Dolby Atmos and visual upgrades to apps like YouTube Music and Spotify.
- Gemini is now available in more than 250 million cars that support Android Auto, with Gemini Intelligence and Magic Q coming to Android Auto for capable phones.
- Google Built-in is available in over 100 car models from 16 brands, including Spotify, HBO Max, and Zoom coming soon, with deep hardware integration like answering questions about the actual car and live lane guidance via the front-facing camera.
- Google Book is a new category of laptops combining Android — with apps from Google Play — and Chrome OS — with the world's most popular browser and the biggest library of extensions — and is the first laptop designed for Gemini Intelligence from the ground up.
- Magic Pointer is a new cursor that activates with a wiggle and surfaces contextual, multimodal suggestions, demonstrated by combining a nursery photo, a wallpaper, and a crib image.
- Create my widget is also on Google Book, with a family reunion planner showing flights, hotel information, restaurant reservations, and a countdown.
- Google Book lets users access phone apps directly on the laptop without picking up the phone, demonstrated with Duolingo, and includes Quick Access for files on a connected phone.
- HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASA, and ASUS are partners, with every Google Book identified by a unique mark called the Global, available later this year.
- Updates are coming to devices with Android 17 this summer, starting with Samsung and Google Pixel, with a developers cut following the main show and more Android news next week at Google IO.
